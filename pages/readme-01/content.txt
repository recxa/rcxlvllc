rcx01
sample keyboard
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
----------
a keyboard-only interface for making beats with friends. i built it because
whenever we'd hang out and want to record something, i was the only one who
knew FL studio - so i'd be glued to the laptop while everyone else just waited.
i wanted something where we could all be at the wheel of the beat together.


HOW IT WORKS
------------
the whole thing runs on a single keyboard. no mouse, no menus.

tempo is controlled by flying a little ship around a 2d plane - think asteroids.
the ship's position maps to BPM. the keyboard splits into two 4x4 grids:
one for picking which loops are active (drums, samples), the other for setting
and triggering jump points - spots you can snap the playhead to on the fly.

recording works the same way. hold tab to flip the right grid into rec mode.
press a slot to arm it, then record vocals. whatever you record stays locked to
the beat even if you change tempo mid-take - like recording onto spinning vinyl.


CONTROLS
--------
arrows          fly the tempo ship (up=thrust, left/right=turn)
space           brake

1-4 / q-r       sample grid (left 4x4)
a-f / z-v       drum grid (left 4x4)

5-8 / t-u       jump grid (right 4x4)
g-k / b-,       jump grid (right 4x4)

[ / ]           reverse playback (x / y axis)
- / =           mute deck 1 / deck 2

tab + grid      recording mode
enter           arm/disarm recording
backspace       delete jump or recording


WHY
---
when only one person knows the tools, they become a middleman between
everyone else and the music. these gaps aren't about talent or creativity -
they're about interface. by mapping beat manipulation to a vocabulary that
everyone in the room can learn in five minutes, this thing dissolves the
barrier between people and the beat.


USES: processing, supercollider, osc
