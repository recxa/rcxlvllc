rcx01

dynamic loop sampler

a keyboard-only sampler inspired by video game control philosophy. the meat of the keyboard is segmented into two 4x4 grids. the left grid controls sample sources, each row corresponding to a drum loop and each column an instrumental loop. the x,y position of your keyboard input within the grid determines the active sample-source combination. when multiple samples overlap, they are meshed together using non-negative matrix factorization. the right grid is a bank of 16 jump points, which the user can set, trigger, or clear at any time. jump points can be set for drum and instrument tracks jointly or independently, allowing for improvised yet complex sample flips. tempo is controlled by flying an asteroids-style ship on a 2d plane with arrow keys. the user can record vocal loops at any time, even while changing tempo, and it will remain rhythmically and harmonically synced to the sample as if recorded directly onto a spinning vinyl record (with the side effect of a natural formant shift).

initial prototype — 12/04/23

the first working prototype. performed on laptop by me and my friends at a gyro shop after concert. all music in this demo is original.

recent version

a more recent iteration, recorded at my partner's pizzeria. here we see jump, waveform, and blend functionality. i am experimenting with jump point quantization intervals.

playtest study

a formal study of 9 participants using the instrument with no prior instructions. using keylog and screen capture analysis, i evaluate the 'discoverability' of each control scheme. some features are discovered and understood immediately, others are stumbled upon by accident, others are never found at all. in response to this, i adjusted the problematic keybinds and introduced more visual feedback, yielding improved results in subsequent informal tests.

currently, i am working on a back-end system that utilizes stem separation and a LoopMaker-inspired segmentation to give users a one-click pipeline for loading new samples without any DAW experience. for more information, see my draft paper for the downbeat detection component of that system.

rcx02

recursive youtube worm

a real-time AV installation that recursively scrapes youtube for recently uploaded 0-view videos. the setup consists of a TV on a wooden desk, and a styrofoam head affixed to a wooden stick, duct taped to a wooden chair in front of the desk. a webcam is dug into the eye region of the head facing the TV, creating a feedback loop backdrop. videos are foreground-extracted and displayed on top of the head-cam backdrop, allowing multiple videos to overlap. using OCR, the webcam scans the screen for text, which it uses as the search prompt for the next youtube scrape. another webcam sits on top of the TV, pointed down at the desk where an open bag of bananagrams lies. passerbys are encouraged to steer the feedback loop by spelling out words or phrases on the desk with the bananagram tiles, which are foreground-extracted and thrown into the visual mix with the rest of the video feeds, incorporating itself into the OCR strings. i am currently saving up for a used computer that can host a persistent livestream of the program.

rcx03

game of life spectral filter

a spectral filter controlled by conway's game of life. a 32×32 grid maps directly to 1024 FFT bins—alive cells pass audio through, dead cells gate it out. the cellular automaton evolves, and the frequency spectrum evolves with it. two modes: scanning (playhead sweeps through the grid like a sequencer) and full-spectrum (entire grid as a comb-like mask). processing handles the simulation; max/msp handles the audio.

rcx05

noclip — depth-based video compositing

a video composition effect that superimposes multiple video in 3D space using AI-extracted depth maps. for every pixel, the algorithm sources from whichever video is "closest to the camera" at that pixel and frame, as if they occupied the same space and obstructed one another. objects from different videos appear to pass through one another like noclipping through a video game map. the experimental prototype is built in processing, but i later made a touchdesigner patch that massively simplifies the process and improves the masking fidelity.
